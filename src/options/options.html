      Images Experimental

      An image model on this device looks at the pictures themselves — no caption needed — and blurs those that clearly show a topic. Works best for things you can see (animals, sports, vehicles, food, coins); abstract topics such as politics are rarely recognisable from a picture alone, and similar-looking subjects (football and rugby) can be confused. Uses noticeably more memory (about 0.4 GB) and CPU: a fraction of a second per picture on a typical laptop, more on older computers. Nothing is uploaded.

      Most sites serve pictures from separate image servers, and the browser does not let ClearScroll read those pixels in the page. With this on, ClearScroll downloads one more copy of such a picture from the address the page itself uses — without your cookies — and analyses it on this device; that image server sees an extra request. With it off, those pictures are left unchecked and ClearScroll makes no network requests at all.
